How This Portfolio Works

Every lab entry will answer three questions about my design process:

What did the process require?
Every additive manufacturing technology has design rules: constraints imposed by the machine, the material, and the process itself.

What did I design and why?
What choices did I make? What geometry, orientation, tolerance, or material decision was specific to the process I was designing for?

What did I learn?
What worked? What failed? What would I change if I printed it again?
