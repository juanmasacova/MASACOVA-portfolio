# L2 – Truss Stress Analysis

8/25/2026
**Individual Research: DfAM**
One design rule is that overhangs should generally be kept at less than 30 degrees from their base. This is because when material is deposited on the previous layer it's still hot, which will prevent it from staying in the deposition coordinates, fall, and fail to hold the next layer. If you cannot prevent this either by changing the angle or position of the part, you should add supports for the overhang.

Source:
Mani, M., Jee, J., and Witherell, P., "Design Rules for Additive Manufacturing: A Categorization," Figure 1 (Table of Standard Elements and Design Rules, reproduced from Adam & Zimmer, 2014)
